About MTA Istanbul Trading

MTA Istanbul Trading connects the distinctive character of Turkish craftsmanship with customers around the world. Based in Istanbul, we source and carefully select a varied collection of Turkish-made goods, including decorative glass and mosaic pieces, traditional textiles and gobelin cushions, lamps, and thoughtfully chosen accessories.

Our focus is on sharing products that reflect the creativity, heritage and everyday artistry of Türkiye. We aim to make discovering and ordering these pieces from abroad simple and personal, with attentive service and clear communication at every step.
