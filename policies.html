6. Returns, Right of Withdrawal & Refunds

Consumers in Türkiye: Once sales begin, for distance contracts covered by Turkish consumer law, consumers generally have a 14-day right of withdrawal without giving a reason or paying a penalty. The period for goods generally starts on delivery. The customer may notify us of withdrawal through a clear written statement sent by email or another durable medium. Statutory exceptions apply only where the law provides them.

Consumers in the EU/EEA, where applicable: Consumers may have a statutory 14-day withdrawal period for online purchases, subject to applicable national law and statutory exceptions. The customer is responsible for return postage during a change-of-mind withdrawal only where the law permits and the required information was provided in advance. Defective or non-conforming goods remain subject to mandatory legal remedies.

How to request a return: Email mtaistanbult@gmail.com with your order reference, the item(s) concerned and whether you are exercising a statutory withdrawal right or reporting a defect. We will reply with return instructions and the correct return address. Do not send goods to an address obtained from an unofficial source.

Condition and inspection: A consumer may handle goods to the extent reasonably necessary to establish their nature, characteristics and functioning. Any deduction or liability for diminished value will be applied only where permitted by applicable law.

Exceptions: The right of withdrawal does not apply to certain categories specified by law. Depending on the applicable law, this may include goods made to the consumer’s specifications or clearly personalised, and other statutory exceptions. We will not apply an exception merely because an item is handmade.

Refunds: Where a statutory withdrawal right applies, refunds will be made within the legally required period and using the original payment method unless the customer expressly agrees otherwise and the law permits. Under Turkish distance-contract rules, the seller generally refunds payments within 14 days after receiving the withdrawal notice, subject to the statutory rules on return of goods and reimbursement. This policy does not reduce remedies for defective, damaged or incorrectly supplied goods.
